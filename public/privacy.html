Privacy

Responsible data handling for authorized scans

Sentinel Scan is designed for passive, authorized website security reviews. The platform stores only the account, scan, finding, and report data needed to provide authenticated dashboard access, scan history, and downloadable reports.

Account Data

Names, email addresses, authentication provider metadata, hashed passwords, and JWT session state are used to protect access to user-specific scan history.

Scan Data

Target URLs, timestamps, passive findings, evidence snippets, risk scores, crawler coverage, and report exports may be stored for later review.

JWT Sessions

JWTs are stored client-side by the browser and sent to protected API routes. Tokens should be treated as sensitive session credentials.

Passive Architecture

The scanner does not exploit vulnerabilities, brute force content, fuzz inputs, or perform denial-of-service behavior. Findings are heuristic and should be validated manually.

Authorization

Users are responsible for scanning only websites they own or have explicit permission to assess. Unauthorized scanning may violate laws, policies, or terms of service.

Retention

Scan history and exported report records remain available to the authenticated user until removed through future retention controls or database maintenance.

Disclaimer

Sentinel Scan provides educational and defensive security intelligence. Reports are not legal advice, compliance certification, or a guarantee that an application is secure. Always validate findings with qualified security review before remediation or disclosure decisions.
